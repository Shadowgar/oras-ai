# Product Requirements

- **PR-001:** Provide authenticated ORAS members with an assistant for ORAS and astronomy.
- **PR-002:** Anonymous/public visitors do not receive AI chat at initial release.
- **PR-003:** ORAS AI is not a general-purpose ChatGPT replacement.
- **PR-004:** Members can ask natural-language questions about ORAS membership, events, observatory use, equipment, policies, facilities, passes, and website navigation.
- **PR-005:** Members can ask general astronomy, astrophysics, observing, telescope, astrophotography, and space-science questions.
- **PR-006:** Current observing questions use current astronomy/weather data.
- **PR-007:** Relevant answers provide canonical ORAS source/action links.
- **PR-008:** Unanswered ORAS support questions can be escalated to Fluent Support after confirmation.
- **PR-009:** Members can submit feedback, bugs, suggestions, or complaints through confirmed Fluent Support workflows.
- **PR-010:** Website knowledge is automatically discovered/synchronized to minimize manual Q&A entry.
- **PR-011:** Authentication, quotas, limits, caching, and efficient scanning protect API cost.
- **PR-012:** ORAS-specific answers are traceable and do not invent policy/current state.
