# Escalation and Feedback UX

## When to offer escalation

- no authoritative ORAS answer;
- a necessary ORAS live connector cannot resolve a support question;
- member explicitly asks for human help;
- member reports a website/facility/equipment problem.

A purely general astronomy question does not normally need ORAS support escalation.

## Preview before creation

Example:

> I couldn't find a definitive ORAS answer. I can send this to the Observatory team through ORAS Support.

Preview:
- Category: Observatory
- Subject: Guest access question
- Summary: Member wants to know whether two non-member guests may accompany them during a normal observing night.
- Original question included: Yes

Actions:
- Send to ORAS Support
- Cancel / Keep chatting

## Success

> Your question was sent to ORAS Support. Ticket #1234.

## Failure

Explain that the ticket could not be created and provide the normal ORAS support/contact route. Do not silently retry in a loop.

## Feedback

Statements such as:
- "I have an idea";
- "the calendar is broken";
- "the parking area needs better red lighting"

are classified, summarized, previewed, and submitted only after member confirmation.

## Knowledge-gap candidate

Repeated unresolved questions may create an admin candidate containing:
- normalized question;
- count;
- related support tickets;
- relevant resolved answer;
- proposed category/source.

Candidate knowledge is never auto-approved.
