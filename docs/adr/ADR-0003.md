# ADR-0003: Authenticated member-only AI access

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-16
**Accepted:** 2026-08-27

## Context

Anonymous AI access creates spam and uncontrolled API-cost risk.

## Decision

Initial production AI access is limited to authenticated users with any active ORAS membership level. WordPress administrators are separately allowed for administration/testing even without an active membership.

## Consequences

AI becomes a membership benefit; abuse is attributable to accounts; public visitors retain non-AI website/contact paths.

## Reconsider when

Reconsider only when operating evidence, product scope, plugin capability, security/privacy requirements, cost, or deployment constraints materially change the assumptions behind this decision.
