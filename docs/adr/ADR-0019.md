# ADR-0019: Audit and operational observability

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-16
**Accepted:** 2026-08-27

## Context

AI quality/cost failures are difficult to diagnose without operational data.

## Decision

Record scan outcomes, connector errors, usage, rate/domain rejections, and security-relevant admin actions while minimizing raw prompt logging.

## Consequences

Operations gain auditability without defaulting to indefinite conversation surveillance.

## Reconsider when

Reconsider only when operating evidence, product scope, plugin capability, security/privacy requirements, cost, or deployment constraints materially change the assumptions behind this decision.
