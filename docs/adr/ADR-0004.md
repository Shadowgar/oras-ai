# ADR-0004: Two-domain assistant boundary

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-16
**Accepted:** 2026-08-27

## Context

ORAS should not fund a general-purpose chatbot for members.

## Decision

Permit only ORAS/website-related and astronomy-related requests, including reasonable crossover such as astronomy-relevant weather.

## Consequences

Unrelated coding, schoolwork, sports, shopping, generic writing, and other general AI requests are refused.

## Reconsider when

Reconsider only when operating evidence, product scope, plugin capability, security/privacy requirements, cost, or deployment constraints materially change the assumptions behind this decision.
