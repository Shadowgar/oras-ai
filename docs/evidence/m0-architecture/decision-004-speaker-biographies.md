# M0 Decision 004 — Speaker Biography Indexing

**Status:** Resolved during M0 review

## Decision

ORAS speaker records (`oras_speaker`) remain indexed by default as **Event/educational knowledge**, unless an administrator explicitly excludes the source.

Speaker biographies may be used for questions such as:
- who a presenter is;
- a presenter's background or expertise;
- presentation-related context;
- historical presenter information.

Speaker biographies are **not** the authoritative source for current ORAS Board, officer, or committee-chair positions unless that role is independently confirmed by an approved ORAS organizational source.

Deleted or materially changed speaker records follow normal synchronization and retirement rules.

This remains part of the Draft M0 package until the complete M0 architecture is owner-approved and frozen.
