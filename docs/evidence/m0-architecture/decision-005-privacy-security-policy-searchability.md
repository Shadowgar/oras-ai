# M0 Decision 005 — Privacy and Security Policy Searchability

**Status:** Resolved during M0 review

## Decision

Public ORAS privacy and website-security policy pages remain searchable as **Policies & Rules** knowledge.

They are intended to answer questions such as:
- how ORAS handles website/privacy matters;
- where to report a website vulnerability;
- what an ORAS privacy/security policy says;
- related data-handling or legal/terms questions.

## Retrieval rule

These sources use intent-targeted retrieval. They should not be included in ordinary astronomy, event, membership, or observatory evidence packets unless the question is materially related to privacy, security, legal/terms, data handling, or vulnerability reporting.

The sources remain public; the restriction is about retrieval relevance, not access control.

This remains part of the Draft M0 package until the complete M0 architecture is owner-approved and frozen.
