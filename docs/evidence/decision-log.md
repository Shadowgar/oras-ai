# Product Discovery Decision Log

## 2026-08-16

Product directions established before M0 freeze:

- Build ORAS AI as a custom WordPress integration rather than rely entirely on an off-the-shelf chatbot plugin.
- Automatically scan/synchronize useful website knowledge instead of manually entering every FAQ.
- Treat changing event/product information as live data.
- Integrate existing Fluent Support instead of creating a separate ticket system.
- Initial AI access is members-only to limit spam and token abuse.
- Permit general astronomy questions.
- Prohibit unrelated general-purpose AI requests.
- Support observing-night recommendations combining ORAS availability, sky, and weather.
- Allow Observer Pass/event recommendations and purchase links; checkout stays in normal ORAS commerce flow.

These directions are represented as Proposed ADRs until M0 owner acceptance.
